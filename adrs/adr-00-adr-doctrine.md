# ADR-00 — Architecture Decision Records Doctrine

> Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) capture significant, settled architectural decisions and principles.

1. **Rule Authority:** ADRs document agreed-upon architectural invariants and boundaries for the project.
2. **Positive Guidance:** Prefer affirmative design principles over excessive negative prohibitions whenever possible.
3. **Concise & High-Signal:** ADRs are brief, direct, and capture the WHAT and WHY of architectural decisions, leaving implementation details to code.
4. **Living Records:** When decisions are superseded, record the new decision and mark the predecessor superseded.
