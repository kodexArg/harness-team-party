# harness-team-party

> *Los nombres eran más antiguos que la obra.*

This lintel does not name a product. It names a shape. The letters on it —
`harness-team-party` — are the house's own name, not a kingdom's. Behind the
wood there is an inn, a company, a single living line, and a constitution
whose sentences are still hollow. The slots wait in double braces —
`{{project name}}` among them — and until a later hand fills those hollows,
no one here will invent a market, a user, or a live secret.

What you have entered is a harness. Documentation, doctrine, a Party, and
the discipline that keeps a serious work from forgetting itself. Stack and
cloud are not assumed. A copy of this house may become anything. This copy
has become nothing yet, on purpose.

It was generalized from a living product's harness —
`alvs-financial-gateway` — and from the earlier templates `harness-base` and
`harness-default`. Every product string was emptied. Every stack-specific
skill became a slot. What remains is the Party, the inn, and the single line.

## The inn

You are standing at **The Three Feathers**. Las Tres Plumas. Rain finds the
threshold whether or not anyone is hunting. The hearth is often cold. The
notice board is not. Issues hang there like damp parchment. Pull requests
are the only bridge. The living line is `main`; there is no second
production road beyond it. Every change that lands must cross that bridge.

The Hunter pares the quarry at that board and pins a bulletin for a later
Hunter. He does not love the notice. He does not forge. Doubt is a tool:
he will not believe a lament until a snare already in the grass has spoken.
The Hawk circles the cemetery of old edicts and will not land in the wood.
Five feathers, then perch. The Hound takes the scent he was given and no
seventh herb. Neither writes the bulletin. Neither becomes a builder.

The Bard receives a finished song and alone passes it onto the single line.
He does not mend the ore while singing. Issues are the inn's lanterns, not
his stave.

The longer ballad — rain on the cloak, nine notices, the lantern-eater, the
sealed hop, the Paladin's exact measure — rests in
[A-PARTY-TALE.md](A-PARTY-TALE.md). That parchment is a tale inside the tale.
This sign is not that song. It is the door.

## The company

They keep to their seats. Do not ask them to explain the furniture. The
names were older than the work. The work was older than the names.

The Cleric keeps the interval. He does not walk the kingdoms. He alone
writes the catalog; he is the only hop between the sealed pair. If the
mountain already feeds the canopy, he returns the fruit uncut.

The Dwarf mines what was named, not what was wished. He distrusts a shaft
without a survey and a charge without a snare. The canopy is another
weather. He does not climb it.

The Elf does not invent roads. She arranges what the scroll already permits.
Small vessels in larger vessels. Light means few moving parts, not absence
of covenant. If the fruit is already on the table, she plates it.

The Paladin keeps the rule where the world cannot leak into it. Time enters
as an ordinary parameter. He cuts once. Then he sends for the snare, not
before.

The Trickster plants what must fail before the mountain is honest, and what
must hold after the Paladin has cut. He cannot wear the face. He returns
the wires. He does not green them by rewriting the ore.

The Wizard binds the room so the play can occur. He does not author the
players. A name is not a key.

The Inquisitor names the written church against the deed. He changes no
character. A pile of findings is not a license to become a builder.

The Adventurer walks one eligible road alone. Three brass weights, their
sum under five, none above two: then the road is his and no companion's.
If a border appears, he stops and names the owner. He does not smuggle a
kingdom in a knapsack.

The Owl reads the index of permitted scrolls before it leaves the rafters.
It fetches what is listed. If the row is missing it names The Crow and
folds its wings.

The Crow is released once into pages that were never consecrated. It does
not choose the temple. It does not force a lock. After the pack, it is
spent. There is no second dive.

The Liturgist sits with a gray candle and is not a worker. He keeps the
phrases. He grants nothing. A parent that summons him for a page, a hunt,
or a merge has left the map. Do not dispatch him.

In the deepest corner, beyond the fire, a retired silhouette still waits
for a summons history will not send. The archive once marked that outline.
No one gives it a seat.

## The doors

The pale roots of Graphify are the first map, when the graph is present.
After that, the files. The graph will not invent a product any more than
this lintel will.

The constitution is [the PRD](docs/PRD.md). Until its hollows are filled it
asks questions, not answers. The catalog is
[INTERFACES](docs/INTERFACES.md), still a placeholder, still the only
authority a route will ever have. The runtime index is
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). The fill-in map is
[ONBOARDING](docs/ONBOARDING.md). The operator's copy-rite is
[CLONE](docs/CLONE.md). The delivery covenant hangs in
[GITHUB](docs/GITHUB.md). Doctrine lives under [adrs/](adrs/). The rest of
the church is [docs/](docs/). The snares that keep the house honest wait
in [tests/](tests/).

Do not confuse the index with the map. Do not confuse the inn with the
kingdom that has not yet been named. Do not fill a hollow with a wish.

The license is MIT. That is not hospitality. It is permission to copy the
shape.
